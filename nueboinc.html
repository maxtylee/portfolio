Neublox - Educational Fluidic Circuit Building Blocks

2024
  • Rapid Prototyping & CAD Modeling
  • Soft Robotics Design & Integration
  • Fluid Mechanics & Pneumatic Circuitry
  • Experimental Testing & Iteration
  • STEM Education & Technical Communication

Context / Problem 🔍:
Educating children in STEM—specifically in soft robotics and logic circuits—typically relies on electronics, which may be less tactile and more complex for young learners. Neublox aims to introduce 7–12 year olds to computational logic and actuation through safe, hands-on pneumatic circuits.

My Role 🛠️:
Designed and fabricated core pneumatic components including push-to-break buttons, resistor blocks, and soft robotic grippers. Prototyped and iterated through multiple manufacturing methods, including silicone molding and acrylic lamination. Contributed to system integration and kit assembly for user testing and demonstration.

Solution Summary 💡:
Built a modular, electronics-free educational kit that uses air pressure to demonstrate logic gates (NOT, AND, OR, etc.) and actuates robotic mechanisms. The system enables kids to assemble pneumatic circuits that mimic electronic logic and control soft robotic actuators using air instead of electricity.

Technical Highlights 🔧:

  • Manufactured push-to-break buttons using silicone molding and 3D-printed molds, tested to operate reliably at 10 psi.
  • Engineered pneumatic resistors with tunable airflow and calculated pressure drop using fluid dynamics equations.
  • Used SolidWorks to design logic gate blocks, grippers, and connector geometries for manufacturability and child safety.
  • Prototyped and validated pneumatic NOT, AND, OR, NAND, and NOR gates through iterative testing and performance analysis.
  • Wrote educational content and example pneumatic circuits demonstrating logic gate behavior for children; developed manufacturing guidelines for scalable production.
